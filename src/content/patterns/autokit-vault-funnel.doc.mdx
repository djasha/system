## Prompt

Fifth surface on the funnel skeleton, first for a membership: vault-plum accent layer, a vault-door mark with a turning dial and a key, and a shelf section (per-kit color-keyed cards) replacing the parts grid. The narrative sells the all-access trade honestly: kits stay complete and separately purchasable forever; the membership adds the whole shelf, future kits at release, and a complete monthly drop. The anti-drip house rule is printed in the ticket section so buyers can hold the brand to it.

## Usage

- Live example at `/autokit/funnels/autokit-workflow-vault/` (noindex — production ships from botbento.com/vault when verified).
- **Status honesty**: marquee reads "opens when verified". Pricing ($19/mo, $190/yr) is DRAFT; the Gumroad draft exists but recurrence must be configured in the UI before publish, and the Vault never launches with an empty drop queue (VAULT-SPEC.md rule).
- The shelf card colors are each kit's accent — the design system's per-bundle theming shown on one page.
- Caught by the factory QA gate pre-publish: honesty copy quoting a banned urgency phrase verbatim — reworded. Run `autokit-factory/qa-gate.sh` before shipping funnel changes.

## Do / Don't

- **Do** keep the "kits never go subscription-only" promise in the pricing section — it's the trust anchor.
- **Do** update the shelf section every time a kit ships; it must always match the registry.
- **Don't** publish while the drop queue is empty — a membership with no rhythm is a refund machine.
- **Don't** use countdown or scarcity mechanics — membership pressure tactics violate the house voice.
