## Prompt

Build an A4 portrait cover template for numbered guides in a boxed kit: paper page with tri-color gradient keyline, mono uppercase top row (kit name + edition), a giant outlined ghost numeral bleeding off the right edge (220px, transparent fill, 1.5px stroke), extra-bold display title at 52px, muted promise line, short accent rule, then a two-column specs grid — Time, Needs, Result, If-stuck — with dotted separators. Footer pairs the brand mark with a mono file/version block. All content swaps via data-field attributes; print stylesheet removes the backdrop.

## Usage

- One cover per numbered guide; the ghost numeral matches the part number on the box grid.
- The specs grid is a promise — Time and Result must be real, verified numbers ("commands verified" + date in the footer).
- Mascot appears on covers ONLY for OpenClaw-bundle guides (see AutokitLobbyMascot rules).
- Live example at `/autokit/product-artifacts/guide-cover.html`.

## Do / Don't

- **Do** keep titles to a few words with a period — "Gateway checklists." is the voice.
- **Do** fill the If-stuck row with a real section reference, not "contact support".
- **Don't** let the ghost numeral gain a fill; outline-only is the look.
- **Don't** ship a cover whose footer version/date doesn't match the guide inside.
