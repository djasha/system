## Prompt

Build a ticket-shaped card: two-column grid (content + 84px stub), 2px amber border, 22px radius, hard 10px drop shadow in the page shadow color. The stub is separated by a 2px dashed amber border — the perforation — and holds a vertical mono uppercase label (writing-mode: vertical-rl, 0.3em tracking). Main area: mono amber-text kicker, 20px extra-bold display title, 13.5px muted body. Amber signals "extra/bonus" — visually distinct from the red accent used on core CTAs.

## Usage

- Reserve for genuine extras: bonus packs, launch-week inclusions, coupons.
- The kicker states the deal terms plainly ("Included free with launch-week orders") — no fake scarcity.
- One ticket per page section; it loses its "special" reading when repeated.

## Do / Don't

- **Do** keep the stub label to one word ("Bonus", "Coupon").
- **Don't** use the ticket for core product content — that's AutokitPartCard's job.
- **Don't** recolor it to red; amber-vs-red is the bonus-vs-core distinction.
