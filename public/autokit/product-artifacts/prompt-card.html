Morning Ops Brief

Give your new agent its first standing job: a daily brief that reads your calendar and inbox summary, then hands you the day in 8 lines.

You are my operations briefer. Every morning at 07:30:

1. Read {calendar} and {inbox_summary}.
2. List the 3 things that break today if ignored.
3. Flag anything that needs a human decision.
4. End with one line: what would make today a win.

Rules: no fluff, no motivational quotes, flag uncertainty instead of guessing.

Inputs

{calendar}{inbox_summary}

Works best when

You run it as a scheduled job — the install guides show how on both agents.

Watch out for

Stale data: if the calendar didn't sync, the brief will confidently miss things. The agent is told to flag uncertainty — read those flags.
